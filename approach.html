Home/Our approach

The gap isn't technical. It's human.

AI transformation is fundamentally a human challenge, not a technology one. Our nine-pillar framework keeps the deep leadership work human — while AI automates the repeatable.

Score yourself on the pillars
(01) — What we believeHuman-centred AI leadership

The leaders who navigate AI well won't be the ones who understand the technology most. They'll be the ones who lead with the most clarity, humanity and strategic intent.

— Melissa Frerk, founderAbout Melissa
(02) — The framework01 / 09

Nine pillars. One whole leader.

Each pillar is a capability leaders and teams can build. Together they describe an organisation that moves through change with clarity and calm.

Pillar 01 / 09Strategy
01

Strategic clarity

A clear, shared view of where AI genuinely moves your organisation forward — and where it doesn't. Purpose before tools.

“Our leadership team shares a clear view of where AI fits in our strategy, and why.”

Pillar 02 / 09Communication
02

Clear communication

Talking about AI to your board, your team and your clients with honesty and calm — replacing rumour with direction.

“I can explain our approach to AI to my team in a way that builds confidence, not anxiety.”

Pillar 03 / 09Safety
03

Psychological safety

Teams that feel safe to ask the naive question, try the new thing and admit when it isn't working.

“People in my team feel safe to voice concerns or admit they don't understand something about AI.”

Pillar 04 / 09Culture
04

Culture & trust

The norms, rituals and behaviours that decide whether change sticks — or quietly gets ignored.

“Our culture rewards curiosity and experimentation, not just getting it right the first time.”

Pillar 05 / 09Change
05

Change readiness

Reducing fear and resistance before it hardens, with a practical plan people can see themselves in.

“When change is announced here, people generally lean in rather than brace against it.”

Pillar 06 / 09Capability
06

Capability uplift

Building the confidence and everyday fluency your people need to work well alongside AI.

“Our people have had practical support to build confidence using AI in their day-to-day work.”

Pillar 07 / 09Responsibility
07

Responsible AI & risk

Guardrails that protect your people, clients and reputation — without smothering good ideas.

“We have clear, well-understood guidelines for using AI responsibly and safely.”

Pillar 08 / 09Talent
08

Future-fit talent

Rethinking roles, hiring and development around the human capabilities AI can't replace.

“We've thought about how AI changes the skills we hire and develop for.”

Pillar 09 / 09Humanity
09

Human-centred leadership

The deep leadership work stays human. AI automates the repeatable so leaders can do more of what matters.

“As a leader, I protect time for the human work — coaching, listening, deciding — not just the urgent.”
