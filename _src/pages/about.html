(01) — Meet MelissaFounder · Chief Enthusiasm Engineer

Senior coach, facilitator & learning designer.

Melissa has spent over 20 years working with New Zealand business leaders on the human side of transformation — communication, culture, psychological safety and change readiness.

Coming from a finance background — and having founded and sold a cutting-edge tech business — she knows first-hand what it takes to lead through risk, growth and change.

She founded Polish to address the human side of AI transformation, and to give every leader access to the strategic, grounded support that used to only exist in the room.
