(06) — Before you bookCoaching questions

Good questions.

“We don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. We start by understanding your why.”
Is coaching confidential?

Completely. What you discuss stays between you and Melissa. Where your organisation is funding coaching, we agree up front — with you — what (if anything) is shared, usually limited to attendance and the broad goals you've chosen.

Do we meet in person or online?

Whichever works best. Melissa coaches in person across Auckland and online for leaders elsewhere in New Zealand and Australia. Many leaders mix the two.

How is coaching different from your programmes?

Programmes build shared capability across a group. Coaching is entirely about you — your context, your decisions, your pace. Many organisations pair a programme for the wider team with coaching for the leaders driving the change.

Can my organisation pay for coaching?

Yes — most coaching is funded by the leader's organisation, and we invoice directly. If you're not sure how to make the case internally, we can help you frame it on your discovery call.

What if it isn't the right fit?

Then we'll tell you, and point you to something that is. The discovery conversation exists so both of us can make a good decision — no obligation, no hard sell.
