(05) — The framework01 / 09

Nine pillars. One human leader.

Deep leadership work stays human, while AI automates the repeatable. Every Polish programme is built on these nine pillars.

Pillar 01 / 09Strategy
01

Strategic clarity

A clear, shared view of where AI genuinely moves your organisation forward — and where it doesn't. Purpose before tools.

“Our leadership team shares a clear view of where AI fits in our strategy, and why.”

Pillar 02 / 09Communication
02

Clear communication

Talking about AI to your board, your team and your clients with honesty and calm — replacing rumour with direction.

“I can explain our approach to AI to my team in a way that builds confidence, not anxiety.”

Pillar 03 / 09Safety
03

Psychological safety

Teams that feel safe to ask the naive question, try the new thing and admit when it isn't working.

“People in my team feel safe to voice concerns or admit they don't understand something about AI.”

Pillar 04 / 09Culture
04

Culture & trust

The norms, rituals and behaviours that decide whether change sticks — or quietly gets ignored.

“Our culture rewards curiosity and experimentation, not just getting it right the first time.”

Pillar 05 / 09Change
05

Change readiness

Reducing fear and resistance before it hardens, with a practical plan people can see themselves in.

“When change is announced here, people generally lean in rather than brace against it.”

Pillar 06 / 09Capability
06

Capability uplift

Building the confidence and everyday fluency your people need to work well alongside AI.

“Our people have had practical support to build confidence using AI in their day-to-day work.”

Pillar 07 / 09Responsibility
07

Responsible AI & risk

Guardrails that protect your people, clients and reputation — without smothering good ideas.

“We have clear, well-understood guidelines for using AI responsibly and safely.”

Pillar 08 / 09Talent
08

Future-fit talent

Rethinking roles, hiring and development around the human capabilities AI can't replace.

“We've thought about how AI changes the skills we hire and develop for.”

Pillar 09 / 09Humanity
09

Human-centred leadership

The deep leadership work stays human. AI automates the repeatable so leaders can do more of what matters.

“As a leader, I protect time for the human work — coaching, listening, deciding — not just the urgent.”

(11) — Common questionsEverything you need to know

Good questions.

Can't see yours? Ask us directly

What is Polish?

Polish is a New Zealand leadership development company founded by Melissa Frerk. We help leaders, teams and businesses across New Zealand and Australia build the human capability needed to lead through AI-driven change — focusing on leadership impact, communication and culture, not technical AI skills.

What happens in a discovery conversation?

It's a relaxed, confidential 30 minutes with Melissa. We talk about where you are, what you'd like to change and what a great outcome looks like — then recommend the right next step, whether that's coaching, a programme or something else entirely. There's no obligation.

Is the training eligible for RBP co-funding?

Yes. Leading Through AI-Driven Change is eligible for 50% co-funding through New Zealand's Regional Business Partner (RBP) Network, reducing the cost to approximately NZ $497.50 + GST per person for eligible businesses.

Where does Polish work?

Polish is based in Auckland and works with organisations nationally and across Australia — with business chamber networks, RBP-eligible companies and individual organisations. Coaching is available in person in Auckland or online.

How is Polish different from other AI training?

Polish is built for leaders, not technologists. Our programmes are grounded in real New Zealand business context, delivered in formats that fit executive schedules, and designed to produce measurable outcomes — not just AI awareness.
