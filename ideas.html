(01) — Seven ideasEach one live on this site
01

A dedicated executive coaching page

Coaching is your most personal, highest-value offer. Leaders searching “executive coach Auckland” need a page that speaks to them directly.

Open the coaching page
What we built
Who it's for, the work leaders bring, three engagement shapes, a four-step process, confidentiality FAQs and your own words on starting with “why”.
Why it matters
It ranks for coaching searches and answers a CEO's questions before they book — so the people who reach out are already a good fit.
02

A booking path that qualifies as it books

Four quick steps — focus, role & organisation size, time, details — before a discovery call lands in your diary.

Try the booking flow
What we built
Auckland time with the visitor's own time alongside (handy for Australian clients), a live summary, and “add to calendar” on confirmation.
Why it matters
You see who you're talking to before the call, with no email back-and-forth. Ready to connect to your Google Calendar or Cal.com.
03

An AI leadership readiness check

Nine statements, one per pillar, three minutes — an instant score, radar profile and next step.

Take the check
What we built
The result flows into the booking form, so the call starts where the leader actually is.
Why it matters
It turns visitors who aren't ready to “get in touch” into warm leads — and it's easy to share in chamber and RBP networks.

Your nine pillars, made visible

The framework is your clearest differentiator — on the current site it's mentioned, not shown.

“Flip the thought” — a ten-second taste of coaching
